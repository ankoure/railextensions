## Alignment

SacRT has proposed extending the Green Line roughly 12.5 miles north from downtown Sacramento through North Natomas to Sacramento International Airport, crossing the American River on a new bridge with elevated structures for much of the route. Estimated at around $2 billion, the extension currently has no committed local funding source, and SacRT is weighing a phased approach that would start with bus rapid transit on the same corridor ahead of eventual light rail construction.

## Updates

### 2025-06 — Advocacy group outlines project status and funding gap
Sacramento Transit Advocates and Riders summarized the extension's early planning status, noting the project cannot proceed to construction without a secured local funding match. (Green Line service has separately been suspended since June 2025 for construction of an infill station at 7th & Railyards — unrelated to the airport extension itself.)
