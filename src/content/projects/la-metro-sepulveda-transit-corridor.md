## Alignment

The Sepulveda Transit Corridor would add a fully underground, automated heavy-rail line running approximately 13 miles from the Van Nuys Metrolink Station in the San Fernando Valley, beneath the Sepulveda Pass, to the E (Expo) Line at Sepulveda on LA's Westside, with seven stations connecting to the G Line busway on Van Nuys Boulevard and serving UCLA and Westwood. Tunnels would reach depths of 500 feet or more in places to pass beneath the Santa Monica Mountains.

In January 2026, the Metro Board selected "Modified Alternative 5" — a fully tunneled heavy-rail alignment — as the project's Locally Preferred Alternative, with a total cost estimate of $20–24.2 billion including FTA contingency. An initial operating segment linking the G Line to the D Line is targeted for 2033–2035.

## Updates

### 2026-04 — Public comment period on PEL Study
Metro opened a public comment period (April 10–May 9, 2026) on the corridor's Planning and Environmental Linkages Study following the January LPA selection.

### 2026-01 — Metro Board selects heavy rail as Locally Preferred Alternative
The Metro Board approved Modified Alternative 5, a fully tunneled heavy-rail subway, as the project's LPA, with a preliminary cost estimate of up to $24 billion.

### 2025-06 — Draft EIR released
Metro released the Draft Environmental Impact Report for the corridor on June 2, 2025, beginning formal environmental review of the alternatives.
