## Alignment

The METRO Blue Line Extension (long known as the Bottineau LRT project) would extend the existing Blue Line light rail approximately 13.4 miles northwest from Target Field Station in downtown Minneapolis through North Minneapolis, Golden Valley, Robbinsdale, and Crystal to Brooklyn Park, adding 11 new stations. The alignment largely follows County Road 81 (Bottineau Boulevard) after an earlier route running alongside BNSF freight tracks was scrapped due to railroad opposition.

The project has an official budget of $3.58 billion. Design work reached 90% completion in May 2026, with 100% design targeted for fall 2026. As of September 2026 the project faced a $224 million local funding gap that must be closed before it can apply for a Full Funding Grant Agreement. The current schedule calls for a possible FFGA in 2027, construction from 2028, and opening in 2033.

## Updates

### 2026-09 — $224 million local funding gap; opening now 2033
Project staff reported that even with proposed cost cuts, the extension faces a $224 million local funding gap, which must be closed before the Metropolitan Council can apply for a Full Funding Grant Agreement. Hennepin County, which has raised its commitment to nearly $2.3 billion, says it has reached its limit and wants the Met Council to cover the rest. The schedule now calls for a federal application in fall 2026, a possible FFGA in 2027, construction from 2028, and opening in 2033.

### 2026-05 — 90% design complete
The project reached the 90% design milestone, with the Metropolitan Council targeting 100% design by fall 2026.

### 2025-08 — FTA signs Administrative Record of Decision
The FTA's signature on the AROD (August 11, 2025) completed environmental review for the project.
